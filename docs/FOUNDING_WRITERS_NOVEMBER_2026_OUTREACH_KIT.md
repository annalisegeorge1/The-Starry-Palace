This is an invitation to try a supervised-beta writing community, **not** separate founding-membership enrollment. No exclusive status, guaranteed readership, creator payment, sponsored placement, badge or obligation to publish. Existing 13+ account rules continue.

## Permission-first message (send personally, not in bulk)
Hi! I enjoyed your [specific real post or story]. I'm building The Starry Palace, a new writing and reading community. I'm inviting a few early writers to try the private drafting and publishing experience and tell me what needs improvement. There is no exclusivity, follower requirement, publishing quota or promise of views. Would you like the link? No pressure.

**Only after they say yes:** Thank you! Here's our Writer's Door: https://thestarrypalace.com/writers. You can explore first, then create a private draft if you wish. The Palace is still in beta, so please keep an independent backup. You can share feedback using https://thestarrypalace.com/beta; you never need to send me a private manuscript.

## Seven-day pilot review
Record counts without reading private manuscripts: individual invitations with affirmative responses; volunteers who successfully save/reopen a private draft; voluntarily published chapters; meaningful feedback; seven-day writer returns; top reported usability blocker. Fix reliability problems first. Make no claim that the 5–8 volunteer target has already been met.

Do not send unsolicited emails automatically, scrape social accounts, create fake testimonials, promise rewards not implemented, or use copyrighted manuscripts to promote without consent.
