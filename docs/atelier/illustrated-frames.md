# Illustrated Atelier frames

24 individually generated transparent raster illustrations replace the recent procedural patterns. Built-in image generation was used, with a separate composition brief for every design; Starlit Amethyst and Opal Dream received a second background-cleanup pass. These are generated illustrations, not claims of hand-drawn work.

## Shared production brief

Square front-view fantasy jewellery illustration with richly modelled metal, fine engraving and distinctive silhouette. Transparent outer background and portrait aperture; no text, portrait or watermark. Optimized WebP files are 768px with transparent padding, quality 94 and full alpha quality. Original paintings and classic vector collection remain unchanged. Legacy procedural sources are retained under this directory and in FineFrameArtworkLegacy.jsx. No frame identities, eligibility logic, data rows or member settings were changed.

## Rendering

The SVG wrapper only embeds an individual raster asset. It does not draw repeated ornament patterns. A per-frame clip-path limits the existing portrait to its painted opening, including broad sculptural pieces. This does not change or upload a member's portrait. No animation is added. Static versioned asset URLs are cacheable. The 24 WebPs total about 4.5 MiB; individual portraits load only their own frame.
