# The Starry Palace · Founding Writers Growth Plan

**Planning date:** 7 October 2026  
**Window:** 60 days from public pilot launch (schedule begins only once editor reliability checks pass).  
**Budget:** $0 paid advertising to begin.  
**Pilot goal:** 30 active, consenting early writers across multiple genres and forms. This is a target, not a current user count or promised outcome.

## The positioning

> A literary home where you can begin privately, share deliberately, and find people who care about your stories. Gather. Have a cup of tea. Write and read with me.

**Do not market it as** another viral ranking app, guaranteed place to get views, guaranteed earnings, guaranteed badges, or a replacement for a writer's personal backup and publishing rights review.

**Offer today:** private drafts, chapter editor, publishing controls, tags/fandom categorization, writing prompts, community rooms, creative challenges and a place to read others' work. Be upfront when a feature remains under review.

## Before inviting the first cohort — release gate

1. Complete the existing [beta playbook](./BETA_RELEASE_PLAYBOOK.md) on real phones, desktops and intermittent internet connections.
2. Verify a writer can sign up, verify email, start a work, write multiple chapters, use tags and fandoms, save, reopen, and publish deliberately without assistance.
3. Verify the writing pad never loses a newer edit to an older autosave. Make an independent backup recommendation clear.
4. Read and check the public Palace Code, copyright/reporting language, moderation and privacy controls. Document how to ask for help.
5. Validate that public story pages and author profiles look readable when shared outside the app.
6. Do not recruit aggressively until these are tested. Writer trust is expensive to recover after data loss.

## Proposed cohort mix

| Group | Pilot invitations | Why invite |
|---|---:|---|
| Original fiction and serials | 10 | Tests chapter publishing and return readership |
| Fanfiction and fandom writers | 7 | Tests correct fandom classification and discovery |
| Poetry, microfiction and literary shorts | 5 | Tests low-friction posting and Ink Duels |
| Fantasy, romance, horror and genre writers | 5 | Tests nuanced tagging and audience fit |
| Comics/illustrated storytelling writers | 3 | Tests cross-format creator needs |
| **Total target** | **30** | **Not an exclusive cap or paid tier** |

The mix is flexible. Invite diverse voices and newcomers; don't require an existing follower count. Authors do not need to move work exclusively to the Palace.

## The early writer experience

**First 10 minutes:** Visit `/writers` → enter Writing Chamber → create a private work and chapter → confirm save and reopen.

**First week:** Finish a profile, add truthful fandom/genre tags, publish if ready, browse two stories and leave thoughtful comments, optionally join a prompt or introduction.

**First month:** Receive a check-in (only if they opted into contact), attend an optional creative gathering, report confusing tools, and make a second contribution if they wish.

Do not demand participation quotas. Quiet writers still belong.

## 60-day programme

### Days 1–7: quality and proof

- Recruit 5–8 volunteer *testers*, not public ambassadors. Ask for honest feedback, not praise.
- Test both Android and desktop; fix any autosave, lost chapter, account, search or mobile blockers.
- Publish the Writer's Door (`/writers`) and a simple explanation of what happens after clicking Start my first draft.
- Prepare 2–3 *consented* sample stories across different kinds of writing. Never create fake author accounts or reviews.
- Establish a private, optional feedback channel and clear volunteer consent. Do not scrape contacts or import private conversations.

### Days 8–14: quiet invitation

- Invite 10–15 individual writers with a short personalised message and a single link to `/writers`. Ask once and respect a no or silence.
- Seek permission from administrators before posting in existing writing groups, fandom forums, Discord servers, Reddit communities or institutional groups.
- Start with Caribbean/Tobago/Trinidad poetry and literary communities as one source, but invite internationally as well. Do not imply endorsement by schools, libraries or groups.
- Host one low-pressure **First Pages Tea** (20–30 minutes, text-based is fine) to discuss beginnings, drafts and editor usability.
- Ask which tools are missing, not whether authors like the colour scheme.

### Days 15–28: first stories, first readers

- Host one optional **Prompt Orrery Night** per week and one small story discussion.
- Invite real readers who enjoy the genres actually published. Match early stories to genuinely interested readers rather than expecting writers to review one another in a forced exchange.
- Encourage writers to share their own story links on their channels if they wish. Provide a plain, copyable link and a short description; no mandatory reposting.
- Feature a rotating **New in the Palace** shelf based on actual recently published stories. Do not promise promotion to anyone or allow payment/points to buy placement.
- With explicit permission, share a few short writer introductions and behind-the-scenes posts.

### Days 29–42: deepen the circle

- If the editor and moderation remain stable, invite a second cohort in genres readers have been requesting.
- Try optional **Poetry & Ink Duel Friday** and **Sunday Reading Tea**. Announce them only after an organizer confirms the event will run.
- Offer a simple peer beta-reading matching *opt-in only*: writers state genre, what feedback they want and boundaries. Do not expose email addresses or private manuscripts.
- Publish a transparent changelog showing which writer suggestions have led to improvements.
- Check seven-day return rates, complaint volume and editor reliability. Fix the worst friction before adding more gamification.

### Days 43–60: prove retention before expansion

- Aim for 30 active pilot writers, at least 10 writers with a published chapter, and meaningful return visits. These are *targets*.
- Ask 5 writers privately why they stayed or left; ask the same of 5 readers.
- Publish a short **Palace Writers' Letter** only to people who opted in, featuring verified events, newly published stories and product fixes.
- Decide whether the Founding Writers Circle should remain a volunteer programme, become a regular membership experience, or pause for technical work.

## Outreach channels and ground rules

| Channel | Approach | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram / BookTok / short videos | Demonstrate the actual writing pad, reading view, prompt tools and story pages | Fake popularity, mass tagging |
| Fandom communities | Ask moderators first; invite feedback relevant to authors' problems | Dropping unsolicited links or harvesting profiles |
| Writing groups, Caribbean literature circles | Offer a free mini-workshop or first-pages reading; secure organizer permission | Claiming institutional partnerships without them |
| Existing writers and friends | Personal invitations, optional small-group beta, referrals without rewards | Pressure to upload private drafts or recruit others |
| Search / own site | Searchable `/writers` page, clear FAQs, published works and author profiles | Claims of guaranteed readership, revenue or exclusivity |
| Opt-in mailing list (later) | Brief monthly writer digest with consent and unsubscribe | Adding signup emails to marketing without permission |

Do not pay for ads until analytics show that most invited writers can complete and trust the first-chapter journey.

## Weekly rituals (only when an organizer is ready)

- **Tuesday — First Pages Tea:** 20–30 minutes, introductions and one optional opening paragraph.
- **Friday — The Ink Duel Salon:** a short prompt with optional fiction, poetry or micro-fiction entries.
- **Sunday — Reading & Kind Words:** member-led recommendations and constructive, voluntary responses.

These are programme ideas, **not currently scheduled events**. Publish actual dates through the site's Events tools only when confirmed.

## Metrics to count without invading privacy

Measure aggregate progress with account-level counts and opt-in survey responses; do not inspect drafts, private messages, login credentials or author manuscripts.

| Metric | Definition | Initial working target |
|---|---|---|
| Invitations accepted | Writer positively opts in / invitations actually delivered | 20% or better |
| First chapter setup | New writer creates a work and its first chapter | 70% of joined writers |
| First published chapter | New writer voluntarily publishes at least one chapter | 10 by day 60 |
| 7-day writer return | New writer returns to writing or a community action in following 7 days | 50% of activated writers |
| Technical trust | Confirmed data-loss blockers | Zero open |
| Early-reader response | Newly published stories with a genuine comment/readers' feedback | Trend upward, not artificially inflated |
| Total active pilot writers | Consenting writers who create/revise/publish or participate meaningfully in 14 days | 30 by day 60 |

Metrics are pilot hypotheses. Adjust targets after collecting real baseline data. Do not display them as live counts or platform achievements.

## Copy that can be used publicly

**Tagline:** Your next chapter belongs here.

**Short invitation:**  
*The Starry Palace is a new literary home for storytellers, poets, fanfiction writers and readers. Begin privately, publish when you're ready, and meet people who love stories. We're welcoming early writers to help us make the experience better. Explore the Palace: https://the-starry-palace.onrender.com/writers*

**Permission-first DM:**  
*Hi! I enjoyed your writing about [specific, genuine detail]. I'm helping build The Starry Palace, a new reading and writing community. We're inviting a few writers to try the private drafting and publishing experience and tell us what needs work—there's no exclusivity or promise of views. Would you be open to a link? Absolutely no pressure.*

**After consent:**  
*Thank you! Here's the Writers' Door: https://the-starry-palace.onrender.com/writers. You can create a private draft first and explore before publishing. We'd especially appreciate honest feedback about the chapter editor, tags and navigation. Please keep your own backup of important work while we test.*

**First-week follow-up (only if invited):**  
*How did the Writing Chamber feel? Was anything confusing, slow or difficult to save? You're welcome to share a bug report without sending us any private writing. Your honest experience is useful, even if you decide the Palace isn't for you.*

## Decision rule

**Keep improving rather than buying growth** if core editor bugs recur, fewer than half of invited writers can create a chapter independently, or writers report difficulty finding their works. Expansion should be earned by a reliable creative experience, not by launch hype.
