# THE STARRY PALACE
## Content Policy
**Last updated:** 17 November 2026 · **Clarification:** Teen romance and historical fiction boundaries  
**Applicability:** This Policy forms part of the published Terms of Service.  
**Contact:** annalise.pierre.28@gmail.com

The Starry Palace is a community for original writing, fanfiction, poetry, comics, artwork, translations and cultural storytelling. We welcome a wide range of genres, perspectives, characters and fictional subjects. **A work will not ordinarily be removed merely because someone considers its ideas, relationships, language or subject matter offensive.** Artistic freedom remains subject to the rights, safety and conduct rules below and applicable law.

This Policy explains which contributions are permitted and how content concerns are reviewed. Read it alongside our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms), [Site Rules](/legal/rules), [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy), [AI Policy](/legal/ai) and [Voluntary Support Rules](/legal/support). The Terms and applicable law prevail if an inconsistency arises. Our minimum account age remains 13, and a work's age restriction does not excuse otherwise prohibited content.

### 1. Creative expression and difficult subject matter

Fiction may portray conflict, prejudice, trauma, violence, death, unhealthy relationships, morally flawed characters, difficult family dynamics or unpopular opinions without necessarily endorsing those things. A story's depiction of fictional wrongdoing is not, by itself, proof that its creator is promoting harm or harassing a real person. The Palace does not remove otherwise compliant fiction simply because it includes a controversial pairing, an unpopular genre, a morally complicated character, an LGBTQ+ relationship, dark subject matter or a depiction of characters behaving badly.

**We distinguish fictional depiction from endorsement.** Moderation should identify the applicable rule and examine context rather than remove a work merely because someone dislikes its romance, ship, fandom, viewpoint or genre. Meaningful reader warnings, age controls, filters and reporting are preferable to a blanket ban where content is otherwise lawful and within our safeguards. These protections do not excuse harassment of real people, exploitation, misrepresentation of real events as fact, or any prohibited content.

Readers should consult ratings, warnings, tags and descriptions before opening works, and use available filters, muting and blocking tools to shape their own experience. Those tools supplement, but do not replace, platform safety rules. Context matters: a fictional scene, a character's speech, an author's note directed at a person, and a targeted message may warrant different moderation responses.

The Palace does not promise to screen every submission before it becomes accessible. Some curated or imported content may be reviewed in advance; other contributions may be reviewed following reports or safety signals. Publication is not a certification that a work is lawful, accurately tagged or free of risks. The Palace cannot promise permanent storage or availability of every work.

### 2. Works and formats welcomed

Members may share original fiction, fanworks, poems, essays about creative subjects, comics, illustrations, permitted translations and other genuine creative or community contributions supported by the relevant feature. **The Palace is not limited to fanworks.** Content should fit the purpose and rules of the area where it is posted; using a story or comic form to flood the service with spam, scams or unrelated advertisements is not permitted.

Works based on an existing franchise should not pretend to be officially endorsed or owned by the franchise's rightsholder. Public-domain editions, Creative Commons material, authorised reprints and the Lost Works collection require a genuine hosting basis and accurate credits. Linking to a work is different from having permission to copy and host it. Preservation interest alone does not create permission to republish.

### 3. Ratings, warnings and required metadata

Creators must make reasonable efforts to choose an accurate available rating, relevant tags, content warnings and applicable work-level age restrictions. Current rating categories include General, Teen, Mature, Explicit and Not rated. If an available field permits a non-specific label such as Not rated, that label communicates uncertainty to readers; it does not waive age controls or make otherwise prohibited material acceptable.

Where a publishing form requires a rating, warning, fandom, language or other metadata field, members must complete it using the options actually available. Do not deliberately mislabel a work to reach underage readers, avoid a warning or mislead the discovery system. Creators may choose a less specific warning only if the platform offers that option; we do not require creators to disclose every plot twist or spoiler.

The Palace may ask a creator to fix inaccurate or missing metadata; add or adjust a supported neutral label where permitted; restrict discovery or access; temporarily hide content pending correction; or take further proportionate action. The applicable minimum reading ages remain subject to the existing rating floors and account-age rules. Readers are responsible for reviewing warnings, but misleading labels may still be moderated.

### 3A. Young romance, historical relationships and child safety

**Writers may explore teenage love and growing up without an automatic censorship rule.** Fiction involving characters under eighteen may portray crushes, dating, love, heartbreak, affection, kissing, identity, questions about sexuality, conversations about intimacy, and the consequences of romantic decisions. These subjects are welcome across different gender identities and orientations. A story does not automatically require a Mature or Explicit rating merely because it concerns teenage romance.

**Non-graphic intimate moments may appear on the page.** Writers are not required to use a fade-to-black for every intimate encounter between teenage characters. A work may address a consensual relationship, closeness, boundaries, uncertainty, a decision about sexual activity, and its non-graphic aftermath, including pregnancy or relationship consequences. What matters is the actual language and presentation, not simply whether intimacy is implied or happens in a scene. **Detailed descriptions of sexual acts or eroticised portrayals involving anyone under eighteen remain prohibited.** Age gaps, maturity, consent, coercion and power differences require contextual assessment; there is no automatic removal simply because one teenager is slightly older or turns eighteen.

**Historical, cultural, fantasy and politically arranged relationships are legitimate fictional subjects.** Writers may portray an adolescent character marrying, being betrothed to, or forming a relationship with an adult in a historical or historically inspired society—for example, a sixteen-year-old in a political marriage. The adult spouse may be a major character, and the relationship may have affection, conflict, loyalty, duty, resentment, pregnancy, family life or complicated feelings. Fiction is permitted to present those perspectives without a mandatory author's condemnation, without portraying every involved adult as a villain, and without being removed solely for the relationship's age difference or historical setting. This permission concerns storytelling, **not endorsement of real-world child marriage or exploitation**.

**Depiction is not promotion.** A story can examine coercion, unequal power, exploitation, grooming, trauma or sexual violence as difficult subjects, and may include non-graphic references to what occurred, its impact, and a survivor's recovery. The Palace does not require a writer to erase difficult history or avoid every uncomfortable relationship. Moderators should evaluate actual content, intent, context and the way the subject is presented, rather than applying a blanket ban to a pairing, setting or theme.

**Non-negotiable safeguards:** The Palace does not permit child sexual abuse material; detailed, graphic or eroticised sexual depictions involving minors; graphic depictions of sexual violence against children; sexualised or exploitative images of minors; or material that solicits, instructs or encourages real-world grooming, abuse or sexual exploitation of children. These prohibitions apply whether participants are peers or adults, whether the fictional relationship is loving or coercive, and whether the setting is historical, modern or fantasy. A rating, tag, marriage, fictional age of adulthood, or reader restriction cannot override them.

Writers should choose ratings and relevant warnings based on what the work actually contains. Themes such as forced marriage, coercion, violence, pregnancy, trauma or pronounced power imbalances may justify warnings or an older readership, but **historical marriage, an age-gap relationship or implied intimacy alone does not automatically require an Explicit rating**. A less restrictive response—such as clearer tags or warnings—should be preferred where the content itself is permitted and a concern can be addressed through metadata. The minimum account age of 13 and existing work-level age restrictions continue to apply.

### 4. Copyright, fanworks and permission to publish

You must have the rights, permission or other lawful basis needed to upload what you share. **Giving credit does not, by itself, authorise copying or publishing someone else's complete work or substantial protected passages.** Do not upload unauthorized copies of books, fanfiction, poems, art, comics, lyrics, scripts, images, translations, recordings or other protected material.

Creating a new work inspired by existing fictional settings or characters is different from reposting the original. Some fanworks may be lawful under particular exceptions or permissions, but fanwork status, non-commercial intent or calling a work transformative do not automatically establish a legal right to host it in every country. We assess rights complaints on the information and applicable law, rather than assuming that every fanwork either infringes or is automatically exempt.

Respect creator-selected permissions for translation, adaptation, co-writing, downloads, redistribution and attribution. A credited download or a public page is not consent to sell, scrape, train an AI model on or republish that work. Report apparent copyright infringement privately to **annalise.pierre.28@gmail.com** with **“Copyright report”** in the subject, identifying the disputed work, URL and reasonable grounds for the complaint. Rights disputes may follow separate legal procedures; see Terms section 7.

### 5. Plagiarism and attribution

Presenting another creator's distinctive wording, illustrations, panels or creative expression as your own without appropriate credit or permission is prohibited. Changing character names, switching a few words, cropping an image or making minor alterations does not automatically make material original or properly licensed.

Similar ideas, tropes, plots, genres, character archetypes and general concepts are not automatically plagiarism. A complaint should distinguish shared ideas from copied protected expression, include examples where reasonably possible and be reviewed in context. Giving credit does not resolve a separate copyright or permission violation. Collaboration and translation credits must be accurate.

### 6. Personal information, pen names and real-person fiction

Members may use pen names and distinct creative identities. Do not expose, connect or speculate publicly about someone's non-public real name, home address, contact information, precise location, private accounts, unpublished messages or other sensitive identifying information without a lawful and appropriate basis. Do not link a member's pen name to an offline or professional identity without permission, even if you think you know who they are.

Fiction featuring public figures or other real people is not automatically impersonation or harassment solely because it depicts a fictional scenario. However, a fictional label does not excuse doxxing, fabricated material presented as verified fact, threats, invasive disclosures, harassment or sexualised content involving minors. Moderators consider context, audience, targeting, potential harm and applicable law. Keep fictional portrayals clearly distinguishable from genuine communications or official statements.

### 7. Impersonation and misleading authority

Do not pretend to be another member, artist, organisation, moderator, rightsholder or public official, or claim false authority or endorsement. Clearly labelled fiction, parody and roleplay ordinarily differ from impersonation when they do not mislead people about who is speaking or seek to defraud, harass or obtain access.

### 8. Harassment, real-world harm and community conduct

The Palace prohibits targeted bullying, stalking, threats, doxxing, sexual harassment, discriminatory attacks directed at people, intimidation, unwanted repeated contact, and campaigns to overwhelm someone with hostile attention. Harassment can occur in stories' notes, comments, reviews, messages, profiles, groups, gifts, outside links and attempts to bypass blocks.

Moderation considers repetition, target, context, likely impact, power imbalances, whether conduct was difficult to avoid and whether it formed part of a larger pattern. Ordinary criticism, disagreement, a negative review or an unpopular fictional portrayal is not automatically harassment. However, using a work or invitation to deliberately target a real individual, especially after they have asked for no contact, can be harassment.

Do not encourage specific, real-world violence or abuse against people or groups, and do not direct credible threats or calls for others to harass someone. Do not weaponise the report system through fabricated evidence, mass-reporting campaigns or repeated bad-faith complaints. Good-faith reports and genuine appeals remain protected under Terms sections 15 and 16.

### 9. Profile images, covers and other visual content

Profile images and other public-facing identity artwork should be suitable for general audiences. Do not use icons or profile imagery depicting explicit sexual acts, genital nudity, exploitative imagery involving minors, or symbols deployed to threaten or promote hatred or violence. This standard applies to publicly displayed identity images even when mature creative works may be permitted behind appropriate restrictions.

Story covers, comics and illustration pages may have different audience settings, but their presentation must still follow the law, rating rules, age-access controls, creator rights and prohibitions in this Policy. Content thumbnails visible before age confirmation may be restricted where needed to protect younger or unprepared viewers. Do not use an image extension to conceal executable code or a malicious file.

### 10. Commercial promotion, voluntary support and spam

Unlike archives that prohibit all commercial promotion, **the Palace allows voluntary external creator-support links** in designated areas, including supported services such as Ko-fi, PayPal and similar providers. Members must follow the [Voluntary Support Rules](/legal/support). A donation does not buy moderation outcomes, votes or special treatment. Do not pressure readers to pay for access described as freely available.

Unsolicited mass advertising, deceptive or malicious links, fraudulent fundraising, phishing, repeated promotional posting and using creative fields principally for spam are prohibited. Any future subscriptions or direct payments will require separate published terms before launch.

### 11. Illegal content, exploitation and technical safety

Content involving child sexual abuse material, **graphic, detailed or eroticised sexual depictions of minors**, graphic depictions of sexual violence against children, sexualised or exploitative imagery of minors, solicitation or promotion of real-world grooming or sexual exploitation, credible threats and other unlawful or predatory conduct is prohibited. An age label, fictional framing, private visibility choice or content warning does not make prohibited material permissible. Fictional depictions of difficult relationships, historical marriage, coercion, abuse or trauma are not automatically prohibited; Section 3A distinguishes non-graphic narrative treatment of those subjects from prohibited exploitation.

Do not post malware, stolen credentials, malicious executables, disguised executable files, unlawful instructions intended to facilitate abuse, or material that creates a serious threat to the security of members or the service. Do not exploit vulnerabilities, bypass access restrictions, distribute another member's private drafts or messages, or use the Palace to facilitate illegal activity. Report security problems privately rather than publishing a working exploit.

### 12. Reports, moderation, corrections and appeals

If you believe content violates this Policy, use the available report controls or contact **annalise.pierre.28@gmail.com** with **“Content policy report”** in the subject line. Include the relevant URL or content identifier, the rule at issue and useful context; do not share unnecessary personal or sensitive information. Reporting content does not automatically establish a violation.

Possible responses include guidance, a request to improve tags or credits, changing a supported label, reduced visibility, temporary hiding, removal or account restrictions proportionate to the concern. Serious safety or legal issues may require immediate action without advance warning. Where reasonable and safe, affected members should receive an explanation and an opportunity to correct an issue. For older material affected by a newly clarified metadata or public-image standard, we will ordinarily provide reasonable notice and a chance to correct it before imposing non-emergency penalties; serious safety, exploitation, legal and security concerns may require immediate action.

A complainant or the subject of an eligible concluded moderation case may request independent review under [Terms section 16](/legal/terms). Restrictions ordinarily remain in effect while an appeal is pending. Appeals are considered privately, and our published [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) governs personal information used in reports and investigations.

**Fair treatment matters:** We do not remove lawful creative work merely because a reader dislikes its fictional content, and we do not promise that any work will be hosted indefinitely. We evaluate actual policy violations, rights and safety needs, and relevant context. This policy may be clarified as the service develops, with notice of material changes under the Terms of Service.
