### A word from the Palace
The Starry Palace is being built as a community where writers, readers, poets, artists and storytellers can create, share, connect and find fellowship without surrendering ownership of their work. I want it to be a safe and warm place, with rules that are open enough for people to understand and fair enough for people to trust. Feedback is always welcome.

Our first principle is simple: **Create freely. Treat people with dignity. Keep power accountable.**

This document explains the conditions for using the Palace, along with the Palace Code and creator-rights rules. The short explanations are meant to be understandable; the more detailed provisions explain what those promises mean in practice. The terms do not take effect until a final version is approved and published with an effective date.

“The Starry Palace”, “the Palace”, “we”, and “us” refer to the operator of thestarrypalace.com, to be identified above before these terms are published. “Members” have registered accounts; “visitors” read or browse without signing in. “Content” includes writing, manuscripts, chapters, comments, artwork, comics, profiles, messages, links and other material contributed to the service.

By accessing or using the service after the effective date, users agree to follow the applicable Terms, Palace Code, feature rules and the Privacy Policy. If a feature has additional rules—for example, a creative competition or a club—those rules apply within that feature, provided they do not override fundamental creator rights, safety protections or applicable law. Applicable laws and non-waivable legal rights always take priority.

### 2. Reading, accounts and eligibility
People may read works marked **Public** without creating an account. Some published works may be marked **Members Only** and are intended for signed-in members. Commenting, following, messaging, posting, entering events, saving account-based reading progress and other interactive features may require an account.

The minimum age to create or use a Palace account is **12 years old**. The account flow asks for a date of birth and blocks accounts that do not meet this minimum. A member's date of birth is stored in a private access-control table, is not shown on a public profile, and is used to check account eligibility and creator-set reader age limits. It is not used for advertising or AI training. Age is self-declared and is not identity-verified, so the controls cannot guarantee that a person will not misstate their age. The Privacy Policy must explain retention, access and deletion before these Terms can take effect.

This baseline applies regardless of a creator's work-level rating or age restriction. Members must provide accurate information where required, protect their sign-in access, and must not impersonate someone else, evade suspensions or access another person's account without permission. Because members aged 12–17 may use the service, the final Privacy Policy and Terms require review for applicable rules concerning minors and parental consent before publication.

We may apply reasonable security checks, prevent suspicious access and limit features to protect members and the service. Accounts are for genuine people and authorised community participation, not for spam, impersonation, automated manipulation or harassment.

Members may publish and participate under a pen name. A legal name is not required on a public profile unless a specific feature or legal obligation requires it. Do not impersonate another person, create accounts to evade a restriction, bypass another member's block, manipulate votes or rewards, or use automated accounts to simulate community participation.

### 3. Drafts, publishing and audience visibility
A private draft is not a published chapter. The Writing Chamber is designed to allow members to create and organise works before deliberately publishing them. Drafts and unpublished chapters are intended to be accessible to their authors and authorised collaborators, rather than the general public. We use account authentication and database access controls intended to support those boundaries; these controls must be maintained and independently checked as the platform develops.

Creators choose available visibility and publication settings. **Public** works may be readable without a sign-in; **Members Only** published works are intended for signed-in members. Publishing makes the work available to the audience selected, not to every person on the internet by default. Creators should review each work's visibility, rating, warnings, download settings, collaborators and scheduled chapters before publication.

Visibility settings cannot guarantee that a reader will not save, screenshot, quote or redistribute content against the rules. Writers should retain personal backups of irreplaceable work; no web editor should be the only copy of a manuscript.

### 4. Your work remains yours
**Publishing in the Palace does not transfer ownership.** Writers and artists keep the copyright and other rights they hold in their original creative work. The Palace does not claim ownership of a member's manuscripts, illustrations, characters or original comics because they are stored or published here.

To operate the service, creators give the Palace a limited, non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free permission to store, reproduce, format, transmit, display and technically process the content **only as reasonably needed** to provide the service, deliver it to the creator's chosen audience, maintain backups, enforce rights and safety rules, and fulfil legal obligations. This is a hosting and operation licence, **not permission to sell a manuscript or use it to train an AI model**. The licence ends when the content is removed, except for lawful recordkeeping, technical backups for a limited operational period, and content already shared or lawfully retained by others. A creator must hold the necessary rights or permissions for the material they upload. Credit must be given when a licence, collaboration arrangement or permission requires it. No member may falsely claim another person's work as their own.

### 5. Creator-controlled permissions and collaborations
Publishing is separate from giving others permission to translate, download, adapt or co-write a work. Members must respect the creator's selected permission settings and any express written agreement.

For translations, the Palace's creator options are **Allowed with Credit**, **Ask First**, and **Not Allowed**. For downloads, options include **Reading Only**, **Ask Me First**, and **Allow Credited Download**. A credited download does not give permission for redistribution, commercial exploitation, model training or adaptation beyond the licence the creator actually granted. Where downloads are permitted, creator and work attribution should travel with the copy.

Co-writers, editors, beta readers, translators and artists should agree on their roles, credit, access and publishing authority. Collaboration access may be limited or revoked according to the applicable feature rules and agreements. A collaborator's technical access does not automatically give them ownership, the right to publish a chapter independently, or the right to train an AI system on the work.

### 6. AI, creative work and training
**The Starry Palace has not implemented, and has no intention of implementing, a feature that uses members' manuscripts or art to feed or train AI models.** We do use AI-assisted coding tools to help build and maintain the website—for example, debugging, identifying issues, improving code and cleaning up layouts. Their purpose is website development and maintenance, not training models on members' creative work. This statement does not mean technical service providers never have access to operational systems; those access arrangements are addressed under privacy and security.

The Palace's achievement badges and some platform artwork were created with generous help from a relative and a friend. Platform illustrations and badges should not be represented as works personally created by the site operator.

The operator and people helping maintain the Palace must not intentionally send members' unpublished writing, private messages, identifiable member data or production database exports to an AI assistant, coding tool, test service or model-training dataset. Use synthetic or properly redacted material for development and troubleshooting. If the Palace later proposes an AI feature that processes member content, explain what it would access, which provider would process it, its purpose and retention before offering a separate, optional opt-in. Member content will not be used for model training without separate permission from the rights holder.

We recognise that writers and readers have different views about AI-assisted and AI-generated writing. We cannot reliably determine whether each piece of submitted work involved AI. Readers may choose what they want to read and support. **No one may submit, upload, paste or feed another person's non-public or copyrighted creative work, artwork, chapters, private messages or other protected content to an AI model or AI service for training, generation, transformation or analysis without the rights holder's permission or another lawful basis.** Public availability alone is not consent.

Members are responsible for respecting other creators' rights, for being truthful about authorship where relevant or required by a feature, and for following any future creator-disclosure rules published in advance. This section does not automatically ban all AI-assisted creative works; more detailed disclosure standards may be adopted following community consultation and notice. The Palace does not sell member manuscripts or art for AI training.

### 7. Fanfiction, translations, classics and Lost Works
We welcome original fiction, fanfiction, poetry, comics, translated works and cultural storytelling within applicable law and the relevant rights of creators and rightsholders. Posting fanfiction does not make a member the owner of the underlying franchise or copyrighted world. Members may not misrepresent affiliation with an original rightsholder or use content they do not have the right to publish.

Translation permission must follow the original creator's chosen option. Permission to translate does not transfer ownership of the original work. Public-domain status, Creative Commons licences, authorised reprints and other hosting rights must be checked rather than assumed. The **Lost Works** archive distinguishes preservation, provenance and hosting permission from ownership: *preservation is not possession*. A work is not automatically public domain merely because it is old, obscure or difficult to locate.

If a rightsholder identifies material that may infringe their rights, they may contact [copyright-report email or form to be confirmed] with a description of the work, the disputed URL and reasonable evidence of ownership or authority. We may restrict disputed material while reviewing the request, seek clarification from the uploader, and restore content where a complaint is not substantiated or applicable law permits it.

### 8. Ratings, warnings and responsible publishing
Creators must make reasonable efforts to select an accurate content rating, appropriate tags and relevant warnings, including when writing may contain mature or distressing themes. A creator can set a minimum reader age of 12, 13, 16 or 18, subject to the minimum associated with the selected content rating. The setting applies to published stories and comics, including direct links, chapters, episodes, pages, comments and reader assets. A creator may choose a higher limit; the Palace blocks readers below that age based on the date of birth they supplied. Age is self-declared and is not identity-verified, so this is an access control and does not guarantee that a reader cannot evade it by lying about their age. This work-level restriction is separate from the 12-year minimum age to create an account. Ratings are content guidance, not rankings of artistic quality. Readers should review a work's details and exercise their own judgement; missing tags or warnings cannot guarantee that a work contains no sensitive material.

Sexualised content involving minors, child sexual exploitation material, grooming, coercive sexual exploitation, credible threats of violence and other unlawful or predatory activity are prohibited. We may remove or escalate serious safety threats without waiting for the ordinary moderation process. Other mature material may be subject to ratings, visibility restrictions and feature-specific rules; an age label is not permission to post otherwise prohibited content.

### 9. Dignity, harassment and everyday conduct
The Palace welcomes different cultures, identities, creative traditions, fandoms and interpretations. Members may disagree about literature, characters, ships, religion, art, culture and criticism; disagreement is not a licence to abuse people.

Do not engage in racism, homophobia, identity-based harassment, dehumanising attacks, bullying, stalking, doxxing, hate speech, threats, sexual harassment, impersonation, intimidation, deliberate invasion of privacy or unwanted repeated contact. Do not encourage others to target a member, bypass a block or use other Palace features or alternate accounts to reach someone who has restricted contact. Respect spoilers and the discussion boundaries of clubs and community rooms.

Members may report misconduct in good faith. Do not knowingly make false allegations, abuse reporting tools or organise mass reporting to punish someone because of a disagreement. Cultural and heritage activities should be approached respectfully and with accurate information rather than as a means to mock or misrepresent a community.

### 10. Privacy, messages and personal boundaries

Do not publish private messages, home addresses, financial details, real identities or other personal information about someone without appropriate permission or lawful justification. Members should not request passwords, payment credentials or sensitive information in community spaces. A separate Privacy Policy must explain what personal data is collected, why, which service providers may access it, how long it is kept and how members may make privacy requests. The Privacy Policy is not replaced by these Terms. Private drafts and messages are not public moderation material or promotional content. Any exceptional support or security access to private content must be limited to the people and information needed for the reported issue, a security incident or a legal obligation, and the final policy must describe actual access controls rather than promise controls the service does not have.

#### Cookies and browser storage
The Palace may use browser storage to keep a requested sign-in session, remember settings such as theme or reading preferences, preserve reading progress, and temporarily save some composer drafts on the device. Browser storage is not the same thing as a server-backed manuscript or a cookie. Clearing it may sign a member out, reset preferences, or remove device-only drafts that have not synced to the account. Members should use a device they trust and keep their own backup of important work.

A review of the application source on 31 October 2026 found Supabase configured to persist sign-in sessions in browser storage and found app features using local browser storage. It found no direct application cookie-writing code or app-level advertising or analytics tags. This source review is not a complete audit of live HTTP responses or hosting and identity-provider settings; it does not rule out cookies set by Cloudflare, Supabase or another provider. The separate Cookie Notice remains a draft until the live cookie and storage inventory is verified.

The Palace does not currently plan to use advertising, behavioural profiling or optional analytics storage. If optional cookies or similar device-storage technologies are introduced, they must be explained in advance and must not be activated before any consent required by applicable law. Where a choice is required, accepting optional technologies and rejecting them should be equally clear, and members should be able to revisit or withdraw that choice. Public reading must not be conditioned on accepting optional tracking.

## ANNEX A — THE PALACE CODE (COMMUNITY CHARTER)
**I. Creative rights:** Your work remains yours. Publishing does not transfer ownership. Translation, download, collaboration, attribution and archive permissions belong to the appropriate rights holders.

**II. Dignity and belonging:** No racism, homophobia, identity-based harassment or dehumanisation. Disagreement about a creative work is allowed; abuse of people is not. Respect blocking and personal boundaries.

**III. Age and safety:** Use appropriate content ratings and warnings. Sexual content involving minors is prohibited. Serious threats, exploitation and predatory behaviour may be addressed immediately.

**IV. Moderation:** Review before punishment where safety allows. Reports begin a review; they are not proof. Restrictions should be proportionate and explained where possible. Serious or repeated violations may warrant removal.

**V. Appeals and restoration:** Members should understand decisions and have a meaningful route to review where applicable. Errors should be corrected and standing restored where appropriate.

**VI. Privacy and messages:** Honour profile, discovery, chat, mute and block settings. Do not treat private messages, private votes or confidential reports as public entertainment.

**VII. Council accountability:** Council power must be limited, recorded and transparent where appropriate. Avoid conflicts of interest. Council elections and badges do not grant an unrestricted right to discipline members.

**VIII. Voluntary support:** Giving and receiving tips is welcome. Ko-fi, PayPal and comparable creator links are permitted in designated places. Support must not be coerced or used to buy special treatment.

**IX. No unpermitted AI feeding:** Do not submit anyone else's protected writing or art to AI systems without permission or another lawful basis. Public availability is not consent. Members retain ownership of their work.

**X. No scraping or access bypass:** Do not harvest, bulk-copy, republish or build a dataset from Palace content without permission. Do not access private drafts, private messages or restricted member information outside the access granted to you. Report suspected security flaws privately.

**XI. Pen names and personal boundaries:** Pen names are welcome. Do not expose another person's real identity, contact details, private messages or other personal information without permission or a lawful reason. Respect blocks, mutes and room boundaries.

**XII. Fair participation:** Do not use bots, duplicate accounts, spam, coercive donation requests or coordinated abuse to influence votes, moderation, competitions, points or rewards. Voluntary support never buys a moderation result or extra vote.
