# THE STARRY PALACE
## Artificial Intelligence Policy
**Effective:** 31 October 2026  
**Last updated:** 31 October 2026  
**Contact:** Annalise Pierre · annalise.pierre.28@gmail.com

The Starry Palace exists to support writers, readers, poets, artists and storytellers. Members keep the rights they own in their creative work. Posting or publishing work in the Palace is not permission for someone else to submit it to an AI system or use it to train a model.

### Member manuscripts and artwork

The Palace does not use members' manuscripts or artwork to train AI models. The Palace does not currently offer a generative AI or AI-analysis feature that processes member submissions. Unpublished writing, private messages, identifiable member information and production database exports must not be placed into external AI coding assistants, test tools or model-training datasets. Development and support work should use source code, synthetic examples or properly redacted material.

The Palace's hosting, authentication, database and storage providers process information to operate the website. That operational processing is separate from training an AI model. If the Palace proposes an AI feature that accesses member content, it will first describe the content involved, the purpose, provider, retention and member choices, and obtain any permission required before the feature is activated.

### Member use of AI

Do not submit another person's non-public or copyrighted writing, artwork, chapters, private messages or other protected material to an AI service for training, generation, transformation or analysis without the rights holder's permission or another lawful basis. Public availability alone is not consent. Follow any applicable creator licence and disclosure rules for competitions, collaborations and submitted work.

The Palace does not currently require a general label on every AI-assisted contribution. A feature may publish its own disclosure rule before accepting entries. Members remain responsible for truthful authorship claims, permissions and any disclosure required by law or a feature's announced rules.

### Questions or reports

For an AI-related privacy or rights concern, email **annalise.pierre.28@gmail.com** with “AI policy” in the subject.
