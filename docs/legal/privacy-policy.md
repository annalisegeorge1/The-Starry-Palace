# THE STARRY PALACE
## Privacy Policy
**Effective:** 31 October 2026  
**Last updated:** 31 October 2026  
**Operator:** Annalise Pierre  
**Privacy contact:** annalise.pierre.28@gmail.com

This policy explains how The Starry Palace handles information when visitors read public pages and when members create an account, write, read, message or take part in community features. The Palace is operated by Annalise Pierre. Send privacy questions or requests to the email above.

### 1. Age and account eligibility

The minimum age to create or use a Palace account is 12. Registration asks for a date of birth, and the account flow rejects a date that does not meet the minimum. Existing members may be asked to enter their date of birth before they continue using an account. The date is self-declared; the Palace does not verify identity or request identity documents for this check.

Creators can set minimum reading ages for published stories and comics. The available floors are 12, 13, 16 and 18, subject to the selected content rating. The Palace uses the account date of birth to apply those limits to the work and its chapters, episodes, pages, comments and reader images. This control cannot prevent someone from lying about their age.

The Palace does not independently verify age or record a separate guardian-consent approval. A child under 13 must not create an account unless prior parental consent required by the laws that apply to them has been obtained. Members aged 13–17 should involve a parent or guardian where local law requires it. The Palace does not knowingly create accounts for people under 12. If you believe an account was created by a child below the minimum, contact us.

### 2. Information we collect

- **Account information:** email address, authentication identifier, sign-in and recovery state, and account settings.
- **Date of birth:** the self-declared date used for the account minimum and creator-selected reader age limits. It is held in a private database table, is removed from temporary sign-up metadata after saving, and is not displayed on public profiles.
- **Profile and community information:** username, display name, profile details, avatar, preferences, posts, comments, messages, follows, blocks, reports and feature activity that members choose to provide or use.
- **Creative content:** manuscripts, chapters, comics, images, captions, comments and other content members create or upload. Drafts are intended to remain available only to their author and authorised collaborators. Publication and visibility settings determine the audience for published work.
- **Browser and device state:** sign-in session state, theme and reading preferences, reading progress, some dismissed notices and some temporary composer drafts stored in the browser.
- **Operational and security information:** information in requests and service logs needed to deliver, secure and troubleshoot the site. Cloudflare provides site delivery and security services; Supabase provides authentication, database and storage services. Their systems may process technical information as part of those services.
- **Payment information:** the Palace does not process card or bank details. If a member follows an external support link, the payment provider handles that transaction under its own terms and privacy policy.

### 3. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; check account age and apply creator-set reading limits; provide writing, reading, comic, messaging and community features; store drafts and deliver published content to its chosen audience; remember settings and reading progress; send account or feature notices; respond to reports and privacy requests; prevent abuse and protect service reliability; and meet legal obligations.

The date of birth is not used for public display, advertising, behavioural profiling or AI model training. The Palace does not use member manuscripts or artwork to train AI models.

### 4. Drafts, messages and AI systems

Unpublished manuscripts, artwork, private messages, identifiable member records and production database exports are not to be submitted to external AI tools for coding, testing, training or troubleshooting. Palace development and support work should use source code, synthetic information or properly redacted examples. The Palace does not currently provide an AI writing or image-analysis feature that processes member submissions. If that changes, the Palace will explain the feature, providers, purpose and retention before offering it, and obtain any separate permission required.

Service providers process information as needed to host, authenticate, store, deliver and secure the Palace. That operational processing is distinct from using member work to train AI models. No online service can promise that data is inaccessible to every authorised administrator or service provider; access should be restricted to operational, support, security or legal needs.

### 5. When information is shared

The Palace uses Cloudflare for website delivery and security, and Supabase for authentication, database and file storage. Those services process information needed to provide their functions. Their own privacy information and service terms explain their practices. Their infrastructure and support locations may be outside a member's country.

Published work and public profile information are visible according to the creator's settings and the selected reader age. A member's date of birth and unpublished drafts are not intended to appear in public profile or discovery responses. The Palace may disclose relevant information where required by law, to respond to a credible safety emergency, or to investigate abuse or a security incident, subject to applicable safeguards.

If a member follows an external payment or social link, that provider receives information under its own policies. A link on the Palace does not mean that the Palace operates or endorses the external service.

### 6. Cookies and browser storage

The Palace application does not currently write cookies directly or use an app-level advertising or analytics tag. The browser may store a sign-in session, preferences, reading progress and some device-local drafts using local or session storage. Those items are not necessarily cookies and may not sync to the account. Clearing browser data can sign a member out, reset preferences or remove local-only text.

Cloudflare, Supabase or a future embedded service may use storage or process request information as part of its own service. The separate Cookie and Browser Storage Notice describes what the Palace has identified. The Palace does not currently use optional advertising or behavioural-tracking storage.

### 7. Retention and deletion requests

The date of birth is kept in the private age record while an account remains active so the Palace can enforce the account minimum and reading limits. It is removed when the account record is deleted. Account, content, report and security information may need different retention periods for service operation, safety, dispute resolution, legal requirements and backups. The Palace does not promise that deletion from the active database immediately removes provider-held backups or operational logs; those may remain under the relevant provider's retention and deletion processes.

To request account closure or deletion, access to your personal information, or correction of inaccurate profile information, email **annalise.pierre.28@gmail.com**. Include the email address associated with your account and describe the request; do not send a password or identity document unless we explain why it is necessary. We may need to verify that a request comes from the account holder. Some information may remain in backups or be retained where needed to resolve a report, protect another member, or comply with law. Public content already copied by another person or third party may not be recoverable.

### 8. Security

The Palace uses authentication, row-level database policies and private storage access rules intended to protect accounts, unpublished work, restricted comic images and member information. The date of birth is not exposed through a member-facing lookup; the age service returns an eligibility result. Security controls reduce risk but cannot guarantee that an online service will never experience an error or incident. Report a suspected account or security problem privately by emailing the address above with “Security report” in the subject.

### 9. Privacy choices and contact

Members may use a pen name and do not need to put a legal name on a public profile. Available settings can control profile visibility, discovery, notifications, messages and contact boundaries. To ask about personal information, correction, account deletion or this policy, contact **Annalise Pierre at annalise.pierre.28@gmail.com**.
