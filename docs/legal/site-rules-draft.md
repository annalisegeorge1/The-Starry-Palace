# THE STARRY PALACE
## Site Rules
**WORKING DRAFT — NOT IN FORCE — NOT PUBLISHED**
**Revised:** 31 October 2026
**Policy contact:** Annalise Pierre · annalise.pierre.28@gmail.com

The Palace welcomes writers, readers, poets, artists and storytellers. These short rules are meant to be easy to find and understand. They summarize the longer Terms of Service and Palace Code; those documents, the Privacy Policy and feature-specific rules should be read together once approved. Nothing here is live policy yet.

### 1. Your identity and your account
The minimum age to create or use a Palace account is 13. Give your accurate date of birth when asked; it is stored privately for account eligibility and creator-set reader age limits, not shown on your public profile. The check is self-declared and cannot verify identity or prevent someone from lying about their age. People under 13 must not create or use a Palace account. Use a pen name if you wish. You do not have to place your legal name on a public profile. Do not impersonate someone, enter another person's account, evade a restriction with another account, or use bots and duplicate accounts to manipulate votes, badges, rewards or community activity.

### 2. Your work stays yours
You keep the rights you own in your writing, art, comics and other creative work. The Palace receives only the limited permission needed to host and deliver work according to the audience and permissions you choose. Respect creator settings for reading, downloading, translation and collaboration. A public page is not permission to republish, scrape, sell, adapt or use the work to train an AI model.

### 3. Keep private work private
Do not try to access, copy or share another member's unpublished drafts, private messages, private profile information or restricted room content. Collaborators may use only the access the creator gave them. Do not send another person's protected or unpublished work to an AI system for generation, analysis, transformation or training without the rights holder's permission or another lawful basis.

The Palace is intended not to use member manuscripts or art to train AI models. People maintaining the website should use synthetic or properly redacted examples in AI coding tools, tests and support reports, not member drafts, messages, private data or production database exports.

### 4. Publish thoughtfully
Choose a suitable rating, tags and content warnings. Writers can set a minimum reader age of 12, 13, 16 or 18 for stories and comics; rating floors apply, and writers may choose a higher age. The access setting applies to published work and its chapters, episodes, pages, comments, tags and comic images, including direct links. Readers below the selected age are blocked based on their self-declared date of birth. This work-level restriction is separate from the 13-year account minimum and cannot prevent a reader from lying about their age. Sexualised content involving minors, child sexual exploitation material, grooming, credible threats and predatory conduct are prohibited. Do not claim that preservation of a work gives you ownership or permission to publish it. Confirm that you have the rights or permission needed for material you upload.

### 5. Treat people with dignity
No racism, homophobia, identity-based harassment, dehumanising attacks, bullying, stalking, doxxing, threats, sexual harassment, intimidation or unwanted repeated contact. Critique the work without targeting the person. Respect blocks, mutes, privacy choices, spoilers and room boundaries. Do not use another feature or account to get around a member's contact boundary.

### 6. Keep the Palace fair
Do not spam, phish, post malicious links, scrape or bulk-download work, exploit a bug, manipulate a poll, or coordinate reports as punishment for disagreement. Do not threaten, guilt or shame anyone into donating. Tips are voluntary and do not buy votes, moderation outcomes, badges or special treatment. Report security problems privately.

### 7. Reports and decisions
Reports start a review; they are not automatic proof. Moderation should be proportionate, documented and reviewable where safety allows. Serious safety issues may require immediate protective action. When practical, members should be told which rule is involved and how to ask for review. Do not retaliate against reporters or people who participate in a review.

### 8. Cookies and device storage
The Palace may use browser storage for a sign-in session, preferences, reading progress and some device-local composer drafts. Clearing it may sign you out or remove device-only information. The Cookie Notice explains cookies and similar technologies; no optional analytics or advertising storage should be activated without clear notice and any consent required by law.

### 9. Voluntary support and external links
External creator-support links are allowed in designated places. Check the destination and its payment terms before sending money. The Palace does not guarantee a third-party fundraiser, and a tip is not automatically a tax-deductible charitable donation. Never post payment passwords, card numbers or banking credentials in Palace spaces.

### 10. Keep feedback and appeals respectful
Members may report problems and appeal eligible moderation decisions through the published route. Do not knowingly submit false reports or abuse an appeal process. Council status, social popularity, points and donations do not give anyone unlimited authority over another member.
