# THE STARRY PALACE
## Voluntary Support and Donation Rules
**Effective:** 31 October 2026  
**Last updated:** 31 October 2026  
**Contact:** Annalise Pierre · annalise.pierre.28@gmail.com

The Palace welcomes voluntary support for writers, artists and community projects. These rules explain how support links may be shared and what a payment does—and does not—mean on The Starry Palace.

### Voluntary creator support

Creators may share external support links in profile, work or other locations the Palace designates, subject to feature availability and safety review. A reader is never required to pay to read work the creator has made freely available on the Palace.

Tips and donations are voluntary. Do not pressure, threaten, guilt or shame someone into paying. Do not claim that payment is required for account access, reading, votes, moderation outcomes, badges, points, titles, rewards or special treatment. Do not use fake identities, misleading fundraising claims, phishing, fraudulent links or solicitations that exploit minors.

### External payment providers

The Palace currently displays external creator-support links; it does not collect or process card or bank details. If a member follows a payment link, the external provider handles the transaction under its own terms, fees, refund and chargeback rules, privacy notice and country eligibility. Check the destination and amount before paying. Never send passwords, card numbers, banking credentials or payment codes in Palace messages.

A tip is not automatically a tax-deductible charitable donation. The Palace does not verify, endorse or guarantee an individual fundraiser, and does not promise that a payment will result in a commission, product, service or reward unless the creator and supporter separately agree to it.

### Palace points and gifts

Palace points, badges, digital gifts, Hearts, Stars, Moons, Crowns and titles are platform features; they are not cash, stored value, investments or charitable receipts. Voluntary support does not buy extra votes or a moderation outcome. If the Palace introduces subscriptions, paywalls or direct payment processing, it will publish additional terms before offering those features.

### Report a concern

To report a suspicious fundraising link or a payment-related safety concern, use the report controls available on the page or email **annalise.pierre.28@gmail.com** with “Support link report” in the subject.
