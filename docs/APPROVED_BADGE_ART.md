# Approved watercolour artwork

The eight user-supplied object sheets are connected to 80 expanded achievement families (400 tier cells). Each sheet has ten rows and five columns, in Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and Emerald order. Exact family IDs and normalized names both resolve to the same artwork so database UUIDs do not break rendering. Existing unlock requirements and ownership records are unchanged. Writing and Legendary families are outside these eight sheets and retain their previous resolver.

The three Courts of Moonlight sheets were redesigned with the built-in image-generation tool, using the supplied character sheets for subject identity and the existing silver Moonlit Tea cup for watercolour style. Exact prompts are in COURT_WATERCOLOUR_PROMPTS.json. Assets are saved under public/assets/palace-courts/courts-1.png through courts-3.png.

The 60 court portrait variations are presented as complete sheets in a dedicated Treasury preview; they are not assigned arbitrary achievement unlocks. Complete-sheet rendering preserves the closely spaced jewels and frame details. Object achievement art receives no character frames or colour filters. No image opens a new tab.

Checks cover 80 family identities, 400 unique in-bounds cells, name-based database resolution and missing-family behavior. Existing suite and production build are run before publishing.
